# Recording checklist — final product footage

The included rehearsal is an eight-scene recording guide with synthetic narration, not a working-product demo. Replace its cards with verified application footage. Its timing is 160 seconds at 1920×1080; final narration should be reviewed or re-recorded by the presenter.

## Before recording

- Obtain a working staging or local environment from D/E with all scenes supported.
- Use distinct browser profiles/sessions for buyer and seller. Have the Sandbox merchant dashboard available beside them.
- Prepare one disposable accepted-and-delivered test deal and a separate pre-capture dispute test deal.
- Use synthetic participant data. Never show `.env`, OAuth headers, client secrets, signing secrets, passwords or personal payment information.
- Verify current agreement amount/currency, invoice ID, order ID, authorization status and delivery evidence. Confirm real buyer approval occurred.
- Complete the release checks and resolve the reported bugs. A health endpoint or fixture does not establish a working journey.

## Capture layout

Record a 1920×1080 canvas. Keep buyer and seller windows readable; enlarge each pane when needed rather than showing illegible text. For the payment scene, show the real application record and matching PayPal Sandbox dashboard. Crop to relevant records, with account details hidden. Use the exact scene actions in `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`.

## Required footage

1. Actual buyer mandate and separate seller mandate.
2. At least one visible negotiation change; demonstrate one-cent over-limit rejection.
3. Both parties accepting one version/hash; then a separate edit-invalidates-consent example if it fits the recording.
4. Real Sandbox invoice/order/authorization with the same agreed amount and currency.
5. Actual private deliverable upload, digest, acceptance and completed capture.
6. Separate dispute deal with capture denied before release.
7. Audit events, accurate disclosure, verified public links.

Do not fabricate a PayPal dashboard, replace real IDs with synthetic fixture labels, or describe authorization as escrow. If a required feature remains missing, explain it as planned; do not splice footage to imply successful execution.

## Audio, edit and publish

- Use the rehearsal to practice scene timing; the final video should be about 2:40 and under three minutes.
- Keep labels legible at 1080p and cursor movements deliberate.
- Adapt the included captions to the exact final narration and timing.
- Watch the exported file from start to finish. Check that successful capture and dispute scenes use different deals.
- Upload the actual completed product video publicly only after reviewing the final content and account destination.
- Open the final YouTube and deployed-demo links while signed out before submitting them.
